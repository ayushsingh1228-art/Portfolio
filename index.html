01 / ABOUT ME

Two halves of how I build.

Curiosity for raw numbers meets care for clean, human-friendly interfaces.

I started out fascinated by two things that usually live in separate rooms: making websites feel crisp and responsive on the frontend, and figuring out what raw, unorganized data is actually trying to say behind the scenes.

As a Computer Science student at Shri Ramswaroop Memorial University, I spend my time bridging both worlds. Whether I’m cleaning erratic CSV rows with Python and Pandas or hand-crafting layouts with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I care about making complex information feel effortless to read and use.

Right now, most of my builder energy goes into ANALYZE—an AI-powered data analytics workspace I’m actively developing—alongside smaller AI experiments and practical web tools.
